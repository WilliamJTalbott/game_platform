Rummy — Final theme: warm slate-black chrome, copper/terracotta accent, green board. Toggle switches light/dark.

One continuous felt: stock & discard piles on the left, shared melds on the right. Every card, pile, and meld is a <button> — interaction is driven by clicking them, and the current turn phase decides what a click does (draw phase → piles are the targets; meld phase → hand cards toggle-select and melds accept lay-offs; discard phase → a hand card click discards). A small confirm popup appears right above the cards when an action needs confirming — shown here mid-meld with 3 cards picked. The feed is the floating bubble below the header, top-right. Melds sit flat by default — a meld shows a slight blue outline only when selected (Alice's set of 9s, a lay-off target). Use the toggle to switch themes.
